# 12-Hour Daily Operating Schedule

This schedule totals approximately 12 hours of English exposure while limiting high-intensity work.

| Time | Block | Mode | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07:00–08:30 | Grammar + sentence production | Deep | 30–50 spoken/written sentences |
| 09:00–10:30 | Intensive listening | Deep | Dictation + transcript analysis + shadowing |
| 10:45–12:15 | Vocabulary / collocations / SRS | Deep | 20 active chunks + reviews |
| 13:15–14:45 | Reading | Medium | Summary + 10 useful expressions |
| 15:00–16:30 | Speaking + pronunciation | Deep | Recorded speaking task |
| 16:45–18:15 | Writing | Deep | Draft → correction → rewrite |
| 19:15–20:45 | Extensive listening / conversation | Medium | 60–90 min English-only input/output |
| 21:00–22:30 | Immersion | Light | English-only entertainment/tech/news |
| 22:30–23:00 | SRS + error log | Light | Review due cards + 3-line journal |

Breaks/meals are outside the 12-hour total shown above.

## Intensive listening protocol (90 min)

1. Listen once without subtitles/transcript.
2. Write the main idea in English.
3. Listen again and transcribe 30–90 seconds.
4. Compare with transcript/subtitles.
5. Mark unknown words, known-but-not-heard words, reductions/linking and stress.
6. Shadow the same section 5 times.
7. Retell it without looking.

## Reading protocol (90 min)

- 20 min: first read, no dictionary unless blocked.
- 25 min: second read, analyse structure and key vocabulary.
- 15 min: capture 10 useful chunks.
- 15 min: write a 100–150 word summary (shorter at A1/A2).
- 15 min: explain the text aloud.

## Speaking protocol (90 min)

- 15 min pronunciation / minimal pairs / rhythm.
- 20 min shadowing.
- 10 min prepare ideas (keywords only).
- 10 min first recording.
- 15 min inspect errors and fillers.
- 10 min second recording.
- 10 min free Q&A with tutor/AI.

## Writing protocol (90 min)

- 10 min plan.
- 25 min first draft.
- 15 min self-edit.
- 15 min correction by tutor/AI.
- 20 min rewrite from scratch.
- 5 min add recurring errors to error log.

## Daily KPI

Check all six:

- [ ] 20 active chunks learned in context
- [ ] ≥30 minutes actual speaking aloud
- [ ] 1 recorded speaking output
- [ ] 1 written output
- [ ] 1 intensive listening item completed
- [ ] SRS/error log completed

## Fatigue rule

If concentration collapses, **do not remove English exposure**. Convert a deep block into graded listening, shadowing, audiobook, documentary, easy reading or conversation. The objective is consistency without destroying the next day.
