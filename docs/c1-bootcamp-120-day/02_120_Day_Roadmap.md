# 120-Day Roadmap — Basic to C1 Attempt

## Structure

- Days 1–28: A1 → A2 foundation
- Days 29–56: A2 → B1 independence
- Days 57–84: B1 → B2 upper-intermediate
- Days 85–112: B2 → C1 advanced control
- Days 113–119: full mocks + targeted repair
- Day 120: final four-skill benchmark

**Gating rule:** if you fail a gate by a large margin, repeat/extend the phase. Do not advance only because the calendar says so.

## Day 120 benchmark

1. Reading & Use of English: official Cambridge C1 sample (timed).
2. Listening: official Cambridge C1 sample (timed).
3. Writing: two tasks under official timing.
4. Speaking: simulate all four parts and record.
5. Compare Day 0 vs Day 120 recordings and writing.
6. Decide: C1-ready / B2+ needs repair / extend B2 by 4–8 weeks.

## Weekly load control

- Days 1–6: normal hard schedule.
- Day 7: still 12 hours of English exposure, but only ~4–5 hours deep work; the rest is testing, correction, graded reading/listening, film/podcast and conversation.
- Every 4th week: reduce new vocabulary from 20 to 10 chunks/day and spend the saved time on retrieval and output.
