# Vocabulary & Collocation System

## Target

Do not chase a raw “number of words”. Build **usable lexical units**.

Default daily target:

- 20 active chunks/collocations
- 20–40 passive words encountered through input
- 5 old difficult items promoted back into active review

At 20 active chunks × 120 days, the theoretical maximum is 2,400 new active chunks. Real retention will be lower; quality matters more than the raw count.

## Good card

Front:

> The team needs to ___ responsibility for the failure.

Back:

> take responsibility for — chịu trách nhiệm về
> Example: Managers should take responsibility for decisions made by their teams.

## Bad card

Front: responsibility
Back: trách nhiệm

## What to capture

Prioritise:

1. high-frequency verbs + noun combinations
2. adjective + noun combinations
3. verb/adjective + preposition
4. discourse chunks
5. phrasal verbs that actually appear in your input
6. academic/professional phrases from B1 onward
7. near-synonym differences from B2 onward

## Review intervals

Suggested SRS logic:

- same day
- +1 day
- +3 days
- +7 days
- +14 days
- +30 days
- +60 days

Use Anki or any SRS; do not manually force this schedule if the app already calculates intervals.

## Daily lexical production drill

For each 20 new chunks:

- say all 20 aloud
- write 10 original sentences
- create a 2-minute story/opinion containing at least 5
- use at least 5 again in the day’s writing

## Weekly pruning

Every Day 7:

- suspend cards that are trivial
- rewrite ambiguous cards
- delete duplicate translations
- mark “leeches” that repeatedly fail
- replace isolated words with sentence/chunk cards

## C1 lexical shift

From Day 85 onward, focus heavily on:

- precision: issue / problem / challenge / obstacle / constraint
- stance: arguably / apparently / to some extent / it is reasonable to assume
- concession: admittedly / nevertheless / notwithstanding
- causality: stem from / give rise to / be attributable to
- evaluation: compelling / questionable / negligible / substantial
- reporting: contend / maintain / acknowledge / imply / highlight
- collocation and register rather than obscure vocabulary
