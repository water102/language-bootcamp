# Weekly Test & CEFR Rubric

Run this every 7th day. Total deep-test time: ~4–5 hours.

## A. Vocabulary retrieval — 30 min

- Randomly sample 50 active chunks from the week.
- Score 1 only if you can produce the chunk in a correct original sentence within ~10 seconds.
- Pass: ≥40/50.

## B. Grammar production — 30 min

For each grammar point of the week:

- 2 affirmative examples
- 1 negative example
- 1 question or transformation
- 1 spontaneous spoken example

Pass: ≥80% accurate without looking at notes.

## C. Listening — 45 min

Use one fresh target-level item.

Score:

- gist
- details
- numbers/names
- attitude/opinion if B1+
- unknown vs known-but-not-heard vocabulary

Target: ≥70%; aim for ≥80% before moving up a level.

## D. Reading — 45 min

Use one fresh target-level text.

Target:

- A1/A2: ≥80%
- B1: ≥75%
- B2: ≥70%
- C1: ≥65–70% during training, then improve under timed conditions

## E. Speaking — 30–45 min

Record one task appropriate to the current level.

Score 0–5 each:

| Dimension | 0 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fluency | cannot sustain | connected with pauses | sustained, flexible |
| Grammar | frequent blocking errors | generally controlled | broad, controlled range |
| Vocabulary | very limited | adequate range | precise, flexible range |
| Pronunciation | often hard to understand | generally intelligible | clear, controlled prosody |
| Coherence | isolated ideas | connected | well structured, nuanced |

## F. Writing — 45–90 min

Score 0–5:

- task completion
- organization/cohesion
- grammar range/accuracy
- vocabulary range/precision
- register

## Advancement rule

Advance when:

- receptive skills are near/above target threshold,
- speaking and writing are not more than one CEFR band behind,
- recurring errors are shrinking rather than accumulating.

If one skill lags badly, reallocate **one 90-minute block/day** to it for the following week.
