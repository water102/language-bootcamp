# Resource Map — Current Core Sources

The bootcamp is platform-agnostic, but these are the recommended anchors.

## 1. British Council LearnEnglish — main CEFR curriculum

Grammar:
https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar

Listening:
https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/listening

Reading:
https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/reading

Writing:
https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/writing

Speaking:
https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/speaking

Level overview/test entry:
https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/level/understand-your-english-level

### Usage

- Days 1–28: A1/A2 sections.
- Days 29–56: B1 sections.
- Days 57–84: B1/B2 sections.
- Days 85–120: B2/C1 listening, reading, writing and C1 grammar; use Cambridge for C1 speaking/exam simulation.

## 2. VOA Learning English — beginner intensive input

Let’s Learn English Level 1:
https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/5644.html

Use heavily during Days 1–21 if real native material is still overwhelming.

## 3. Cambridge Dictionary — dictionary/collocation/pronunciation anchor

https://dictionary.cambridge.org/

Rule: from B1 onward, read the English definition before translating.

## 4. YouGlish — pronunciation in real contexts

https://youglish.com/

Use for:

- stress
- reductions
- linking
- how a phrase sounds in actual speech

## 5. Cambridge C1 Advanced — final benchmark

Exam format:
https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/advanced/format/

Official preparation/sample tests:
https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/advanced/preparation/

Current format includes:

- Reading & Use of English — 1h30
- Writing — 1h30
- Listening — about 40 min
- Speaking — about 15 min per pair

Do not burn through all official sample material in Month 1. Save most of it for Days 85–120.

## 6. Suggested content ladder

### A1/A2
- VOA Learning English
- British Council graded material
- very short comprehensible YouTube clips with English subtitles

### B1
- British Council B1
- learner podcasts
- simple technology/news explainers
- graded short stories

### B2
- normal podcasts with transcript
- documentaries/interviews
- mainstream long-form articles
- technical talks in familiar domains

### C1
- lectures, debates, interviews and panels
- unfamiliar long-form journalism
- research summaries and essays
- Cambridge C1 samples

## Rule for choosing input difficulty

A useful training item is usually one where you understand roughly **70–90%** before intensive analysis.

- >95%: good extensive input, often too easy for intensive study.
- 70–90%: ideal learning zone.
- <60%: often too difficult; downgrade unless the topic is strategically important.
